Mission Statement
1. TO STRENGTHEN THE SOUTH BEACH JEWISH COMMUNITY BY PROMOTING JEWISH PRIDE, STUDY AND CELEBRATION.

2. TO PROVIDE FOR THE SPIRITUAL & MATERIAL NEEDS OF ALL JEWS IN THE COMMUNITY - AND THOSE VISITING - REGARDLESS OF BACKGROUND OR AFFILIATION.

3. TO PROVIDE A WARM COMMUNITY HOME WHERE EVERYONE IS MADE TO FEEL WELCOME AND COMFORTABLE.
