Welcome

Whoever you are, however you daven, you already have a place at our table.

Chabad in South Beach is a synagogue and community center on Meridian Avenue — a block from the beach and a world away from anywhere you have to prove yourself. Everyone is welcome regardless of background, knowledge or affiliation, and there is no membership required to walk in, sit down and eat.

Locals, young professionals, families and the ten thousand visitors a year who find us on a Friday afternoon: this is your Jewish home in Miami Beach.
